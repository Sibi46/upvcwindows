Who We Are

Chromepet-Based uPVC Specialists

Shasthik UPVC Windows and Doors is a Chennai-based fabricator and installer of uPVC windows and doors, operating out of Saraswathipuram, Chromepet. We work directly with homeowners, builders and interior contractors to design window and door solutions suited to the size, style and budget of each project.

Every project starts with an on-site visit and measurement, followed by fabrication to the exact opening size and profile you choose, and finishes with professional installation and site cleanup.
