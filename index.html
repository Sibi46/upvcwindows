Why uPVC

Why Choose uPVC Over Traditional Frames

uPVC (unplasticized polyvinyl chloride) is built to outperform wood and aluminium in Chennai's climate.

Thermal Insulation

Keeps interiors cooler by limiting heat transfer, reducing dependence on air conditioning.

Noise Reduction

Multi-chambered profiles and tight seals cut down outside traffic and street noise.

Weather & Water Resistant

Won't rot, rust, warp or swell — built to handle Chennai's humidity and monsoon rains.

Low Maintenance

No painting or polishing needed — just wipe clean, unlike wood or steel frames.

Strong & Secure

Reinforced multi-point locking systems for better security than standard frames.

Clean, Modern Look

Smooth finish and slim sightlines that suit both contemporary and traditional homes.
